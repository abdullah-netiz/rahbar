# University Fee Structure (2026-27)

## 1. Tuition
Tuition Fees (Fee Structure 2026-27): Tuition fee for undergraduate BS programmes (all disciplines including CS, SE, EE, and BBA) is PKR 10,000 per credit hour. Graduate MS programmes tuition fee is PKR 12,500 per credit hour. Admission fee is PKR 25,000 (one-time non-refundable).

## 2. Other Charges and Refunds
Other University Charges & Refund Policy: Late registration surcharge is PKR 3,000. Official transcript fee is PKR 1,500 per copy. Campus transfer fee is PKR 5,000. Tuition fee is refunded 100% when a course is dropped within the first 10 days of classes, 50% between days 11 and 20, and 0% after day 20.
