# Undergraduate Academic Rules (August 2023 Edition - Superseded)

## 2. Academic Warning (2023)
Undergraduate Academic Rules (2023 Edition - Superseded): Under the August 2023 rules, academic warning was issued when CGPA fell below 1.75, two consecutive warnings resulted in programme dismissal, and attendance requirement was 75%.
