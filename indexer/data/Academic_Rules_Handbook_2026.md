# FAST National University of Computer & Emerging Sciences: Academic Rules Handbook (2026 Edition)

## 1.21 Attendance
Attendance Regulations (Clause 1.21): A student must attend at least 80 percent of the scheduled lectures and lab sessions of each course to be eligible to sit for the final examination. A student who falls below the minimum attendance requirement receives the grade FA (Failure due to Attendance) in that course. The Head of Department may condone absence only for documented medical reasons.

## 5.4 Academic Warning
Academic Warning (Clause 5.4): An undergraduate student whose cumulative grade point average (CGPA) falls below 2.00 at the end of a regular semester is placed on academic warning. A student who remains on academic warning for three consecutive regular semesters is dropped from the university degree programme.

## 3.5 Course Withdrawal
Course Withdrawal (Clause 3.5): An undergraduate student may apply for course withdrawal before the end of the 10th week of classes during a regular semester. A grade of 'W' is assigned on the official transcript, which carries no grade points and does not affect the calculation of semester GPA or CGPA.

## 3.2 Course Repeats
Course Repeats (Clause 3.2): An undergraduate student may repeat any course in which a grade of C- or lower was earned to improve their CGPA. In accordance with the 2026 revised regulations, only the grade obtained in the most recent attempt counts toward cumulative GPA calculation, replacing earlier attempts.

## 2.2 Course Load
Course Load (Clause 2.2): The normal undergraduate course load for a regular semester is 15 to 18 credit hours. A student with a CGPA of 3.00 or higher may be permitted to register for an overload of up to 21 credit hours with prior written approval from the Head of Department.

## 2.1 Maximum Duration
Degree Duration (Clause 2.1): The normal duration of a four-year Bachelor of Science (BS) degree programme is 8 regular semesters. The maximum permissible duration to complete all degree requirements is 7 academic years from the date of admission.

## 6.2 Honours and Medals
Honours and Medals (Clause 6.2): An undergraduate student who earns a semester GPA of 3.50 or higher with a normal course load of at least 15 credit hours and no failing or incomplete grades qualifies for placement on the Dean's Honour List.

## 4.8 Re-checking of Papers
Re-checking of Examination Papers (Clause 4.8): A student may formally apply for re-checking of an examination answer script within 7 days of the declaration of official results by submitting the prescribed fee to the Controller of Examinations. Re-checking is strictly limited to arithmetic totaling errors and unmarked questions.

## 2.4 Prerequisites
Prerequisite Requirements (Clause 2.4): A student must earn a passing grade in all prerequisite courses prior to enrolling in advanced courses. Concurrent registration in a course and its prerequisite is strictly prohibited under university regulations.
