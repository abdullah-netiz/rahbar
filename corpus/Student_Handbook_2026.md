# Student Handbook 2026

## Generative AI Policy
Generative AI Policy: Unauthorized use of Generative AI tools (including ChatGPT, GitHub Copilot, and Claude) in academic coursework, assignments, or examinations without explicit written permission from the course instructor constitutes academic dishonesty and plagiarism under university disciplinary rules.
